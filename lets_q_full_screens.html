Organizer · configures no-show policy per queue, set once when creating it

Photo queue — settings

Cancel immediately — ticket is cancelled on the first no-show; the queuer is notified in-app to re-join with a new ticket.
Defer 5 spots, twice, then cancel — no-show pushes the number back 5 spots and recalls it; after 2 deferrals (3 total call attempts), it auto-cancels.
Hold minutes, twice, then cancel — no-show holds the number for N minutes and recalls it; after 2 holds (3 total call attempts), it auto-cancels.

On the dashboard, calling a number shows "Present" / "Skip" / "No-show" buttons — No-show runs this rule automatically, no manual decision needed in the moment.

Share my phone number — shown on the waiting ticket screen, e.g. "Questions? Call 555-0142." Off by default.
