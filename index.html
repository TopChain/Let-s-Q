PRIVACY PROMISEPUBLIC VERSION

Join a queue without handing over your identity.

For queuers: Let's Q should not ask for a name, phone number, email address, contact list, or social-media login. Your ticket uses only a queue number and a secret code that you choose.
  • One short launch sponsor. A clearly labelled, three-second sponsor screen appears once whenever the app opens. The free version then uses only a small bottom banner.
  • No behavioural advertising. The public version should use direct or contextual sponsors only—not an ad network that profiles people or uses an advertising ID.
  • Minimal ticket data. Store only the queue, ticket number, secret-code verifier, operational timestamps, and optional short request. Delete closed-ticket data on a documented schedule.
  • Anonymous ratings. Host analytics use aggregate rating scores only. Do not include names, contact details, ticket codes, or device identifiers in the report.
  • Optional notifications. If a future waitlist uses notifications, ask first and offer a no-notification option. Do not make notifications required to join.
  • Private notes are optional. Keep them short, show them only to the relevant organizer, and delete them when the ticket closes. Never invite medical, payment, or other sensitive details.

This page is the product promise. Before public release, publish a matching privacy policy and verify that the actual backend, analytics, crash reporting, billing, and advertising setup truly follow it.
