Request deletion of Let’s Q data

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Let’s Q does not require names, email addresses, or phone numbers to join a queue. If you want us to delete queue data connected with a ticket or Host device, you may request it by email.

Email Let’s Q support

What to include

Please include the queue name or short queue code, the approximate date you used the queue, and (if you have it) the ticket number. Do not send your secret code or sensitive information.

What we delete

Optional queue requests are erased when a ticket is served or cancelled. Closed ticket records, closed queues, and anonymous ratings are normally deleted automatically within 30 days. When we can identify an active record, we delete the associated ticket, request, and rating where technically possible and where retention is not required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons. We will confirm the outcome by email.
